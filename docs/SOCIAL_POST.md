**Headline**: Unveiling TigerGraph Sentinel: Autonomous Agentic Fraud Investigation & Next-Best Action Engine 🛡️⚡

### The Problem
Fraud teams at financial institutions are under relentless pressure. When a transaction model flags an alert, human analysts must manually correlate card history, device fingerprints, IP proxies, and past investigations before deciding whether to freeze an account. Most of the time, the funds are already gone—or worse, a legitimate cardholder is wrongly blocked.

### The Solution: Graph-Native Autonomous Agents
We engineered **TigerGraph Sentinel** to automate the entire 8-step investigation lifecycle:
1. **Trigger Ingestion**: Seamlessly ingests risk-score alerts, customer disputes, and analyst anomaly reports.
2. **Deep Graph Traversals**: Leverages @TigerGraph GSQL algorithms (`card_window`, `device_neighbors`, `customer_network`) to uncover multi-card device rings and impossible-travel card cloning.
3. **GraphRAG Case Memory Grounding**: Matches live signals against 5,565 historical closed cases to establish precedent.
4. **Calibrated Uncertainty**: Recognizes that over half of high-scoring transactions are benign home-region spending, avoiding catastrophic customer friction.
5. **Multi-Tier Governance**: Formulates next-best actions enforcing strict approval routing (`auto` for automated actions, `L1` for Team Lead, `L2` for Fraud Manager).
6. **Controlled Policy Actions**: Simulates cardholder verification and step-up authentication before making permanent block decisions.
7. **Automated Regulatory SAR Filing**: Drafts comprehensive 6–12 sentence FinCEN-compliant Suspicious Activity Reports covering Who, What, When, Where, How, and Why.
8. **Dynamic Case Memory Persistence**: Stores newly concluded fraud cases directly into the TigerGraph graph so future investigations can build upon them.
